Name: Little Twin Stars
DescriptionEnglish: Kiki and Lala are two twin stars born on Omoiyari Star in the Yume Star-Cloud. They traveled a long way to Earth to learn how to be the best, shiniest stars they can be. Lala is the eldest, loves to draw and write poems, and is a great cook! Kiki is filled with curiosity about everything, which makes him a little cheeky sometimes.
